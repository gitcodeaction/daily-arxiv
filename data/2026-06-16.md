### [1] [PhoneHarness: Harnessing Phone-Use Agents through Mixed GUI, CLI, and Tool Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14832)

### [2] [Evaluating the Robustness of Proof Autoformalization in Lean 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14867)
*Zhengtao Gui,Sheng Yang,Zhouxing Shi*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文首次系统研究了证明自动形式化模型的鲁棒性，将扰动分为全局扰动（改写证明风格）和局部扰动（修改值、符号或证明步骤），在miniF2F和MATH-500数据集上构建基准测试。评估的七个模型均对全局扰动敏感，且大多无法忠实反映局部扰动。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有证明自动形式化评估主要关注精心策划的数据集中的格式良好的非形式化证明，缺乏对模型在非理想情况下表现的研究。作者认为，一个鲁棒的证明自动形式化器必须在面对与理想形式偏离的非形式化证明时仍能保持忠实性。

Method: 提出两类扰动：(1) 全局扰动：用不同风格改写非形式化证明，形式化结果应保持一致；(2) 局部扰动：改变值、符号或证明步骤（可能是反事实的），鲁棒的模型应忠实反映扰动而非恢复原状。在miniF2F和MATH-500上构建基准，自动测量模型在全局扰动下的稳定性以及在局部扰动下的忠实性。评估了七个近期模型。

Conclusion: 当前证明自动形式化模型缺乏鲁棒性。本文首次系统研究了证明自动形式化模型的鲁棒性评估框架和基准数据集。

Abstract: Proof autoformalization aims to translate a mathematical informal proof written in natural language into a formal proof in a formal language such as Lean~4. Several works have developed LLM-based models for proof autoformalization. However, existing evaluations have typically focused on translating well-formed informal proofs from curated datasets. We argue that a robust proof autoformalizer must remain faithful even for informal proofs that diverge from these idealized ones, and we present the first study on the robustness of proof autoformalization models. We formulate two categories of perturbations and evaluate robustness under each: a global perturbation paraphrases the informal proof in a different style, under which the formalization should remain consistent; a local perturbation alters a value, symbol, or proof step, possibly in a counterfactual way, and a robust formalization should faithfully reflect the perturbation rather than reverting to the original one or inferring a different one on its own. We build a benchmark with both perturbations on miniF2F and MATH-500, and automatically measure how stable a proof autoformalization's correctness is under global perturbations and how faithfully its output reflects local perturbations. We evaluate seven recent models, all of which are sensitive to global perturbations and mostly fail to remain faithful under local perturbations. Code and data are available via https://github.com/ucr-rai/robust-proof-autoformalization.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多跳问答任务中，小语言模型面临上下文窗口限制和token成本压力。现有压缩方法（如截断、字符删除等）会损害推理所需的实体信息，导致性能下降。如何在有限token预算下保留关键推理证据成为一个重要问题。

Abstract: We study context compression for multi-hop question answering with small language models.   <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出Arbitrary Conditionals GPT (AC-GPT)，对标准因果Transformer引入简单修改：保留标准从左到右排序和下一token预测目标，使现有LLM可通过微调适应任意条件。模型在单次前向传播中支持past、future及混合上下文的条件采样与评估。

Conclusion: AC-GPT提供了一种兼容现有LLM的简单方案，在保持标准自回归模型优势的同时实现了任意条件建模能力，兼具理论灵活性与实用价值。

Abstract: Causal Transformers model sequences through an autoregressive factorization of the joint distribution, which enables efficient left-to-right decoding and conditional likelihood computation. However, they cannot tractably sample from or evaluate arbitrary conditionals -- e.g., a block of text conditioned on past and future tokens. Recent work aims to solve this problem through novel architectures, but they often lead to sub-optimal modeling of such conditionals and degraded generations. We propose Arbitrary Conditionals GPT (AC-GPT) which introduces a simple modification to standard causal Transformers to enable evaluating and sampling from arbitrary conditionals -- including past, future, and mixed contexts -- within a single forward pass. Unlike prior approaches, our method preserves the standard left-to-right ordering and next-token prediction objective essential for both strong performance and efficient training on natural language. Crucially, this compatibility allows existing LLMs to be fine-tuned for arbitrary conditioning. Our empirical results indicate that our method outperforms baselines on modeling arbitrary conditionals, without degrading standard left-to-right performance.

### [5] [CoRA: Confidence-Rationale Alignment for Reliable Chain-of-Thought Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14961)
*Juming Xiong,Weixin Liu,Kevin Guo,Congning Ni,Junchao Zhu,Chongyu Qu,Chao Yan,Katherine Brown,Avinash Baidya,Xiang Gao,Bradley Malin,Zhijun Yin*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本研究提出置信度-推理链对齐（confidence-rationale alignment）问题，即模型的答案置信度是否由其生成的推理链支撑。引入基于GRPO的强化学习框架，通过联合奖励答案正确性、置信度和基于评分标准的推理链支持度，在三个数据集上将对齐误差降低26.51%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有CoT推理方法中，高置信度答案可能配有不完整或不支持的推理链，导致看似合理实则错误的输出。现有方法关注答案准确性或校准性，忽视了置信度与推理链之间的一致性问题。

Method: 提出基于GRPO的强化学习框架，奖励机制包含三部分：(1)答案正确性；(2)承诺答案的概率；(3)基于评分标准的推理链支持度（评估依据、连贯性、任务匹配、与答案的关联），评分标准不向评判者透露标准答案。

Result: 在MedQA、MathQA和OpenBookQA三个数据集上，使用三种开源LLM，置信度-推理链对齐误差降低达26.51%，同时保持竞争力的准确率且通常改善了校准性能。

Conclusion: 可靠的CoT推理不仅需要置信的答案，更需要实质性支撑答案的推理链。评分标准引导的强化学习可有效提升置信度与推理链的一致性。

Abstract: Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning can improve LLM performance, but high answer confidence may be misleading when the accompanying CoT rationale is plausible yet incomplete or poorly supported. We study confidence--rationale alignment: whether a model's confidence in its committed answer is justified by its generated rationale. We introduce a GRPO-based reinforcement learning framework that jointly rewards answer correctness, committed-answer probability, and rubric-based rationale support, where the rubric assesses grounding, coherence, task match, and connection to the selected answer without revealing the gold answer to the judge. Across MedQA, MathQA, and OpenBookQA using three open-weight LLMs, our method reduces the confidence--rationale alignment error by up to 26.51% compared with untuned checkpoints, SFT, and correctness-only GRPO, while maintaining competitive accuracy and often improving calibration. These results show that reliable CoT reasoning requires not only confident answers, but rationales that substantively support them.
